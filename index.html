The short version

We hold real data

All 32 clubs' 2026 records, the full Week 10 slate, and 345 official injury-report rows. Internal arithmetic was checked line by line: points for minus points against equals net points for all 32 clubs.

nfl.com standings

We originate our own lines

14 spreads, totals and win probabilities, each with a sigma and a 90% band. Nothing is copied from a book. There is no market comparison anywhere on this site, because we hold no licensed price.

Zero strategies have graded results

Eight strategy specs exist, with gates that fail them for good reasons. The leaderboard is live and empty. Any site that showed you a win rate right now would be inventing it.

See the gates

The biggest gap is the price

Without a licensed market feed we cannot measure edge, closing-line value, or whether a line is any good. That is flag F07, and it blocks the top two reward tiers by construction.
