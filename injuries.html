Why "2nd/3rd/4th string" is not on this page

You asked to know when a backup is replacing a starter. No official NFL source publishes a depth-chart ordinal, a starter flag, or a snap count on the injury report (flag F05). Rather than invent one, this site applies a per-position availability penalty in the line model and labels it as a model constant. Making the depth question answerable needs participation counts — suggestion SG-05.
