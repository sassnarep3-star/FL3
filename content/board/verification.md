# Automated verification log

The output of `scripts/verify.py` on the current data. It is regenerated on every build, so this page
cannot drift from the repo the way a hand-written QA note does.

**Semantics:** `PASS` = identity satisfied. `FAIL` with `Blocks publish = yes` = a transcription or
integrity error (the build aborts). `FAIL` with `Blocks publish = no` = the *official sources disagree
with each other*; the values are still published as read, but marked pricing-ineligible. `WARN` = passes
only within rounding slack, reported rather than tolerated.

## The standing exceptions, in plain language

1. **`V-09b` fails (non-blocking).** The league's offensive passing table and defensive passing table do
   not agree on season totals: 414 TDs thrown vs 415 allowed, 202 INTs thrown vs 200 forced. One of the
   two pages is wrong, or a stat correction landed on one and not the other. We publish both numbers as
   read and forbid those four columns from pricing anything
   ([FLG-025](flagged.html)).
2. **`I-02` is `PARTIAL`.** 33 of 137 verified games are in the repo. Everything distributional is
   labelled provisional until the fetcher runs on a networked host ([FLG-016](flagged.html)).
3. **`V-11` lists rounding-boundary rows.** Five published rates sit exactly on the .x5 boundary. They
   pass on the stated tolerance; the tolerance itself is documented rather than hidden
   (see [Methodology](methodology.html)).

## Fixing a failure

`verify.py` is the contract: add the missing read, or correct the row against the cited page, then
rebuild. A row is never edited to satisfy a check - the check exists to catch exactly that.
