Methodology

Exactly how every number on this site was produced, so a reviewer can re-run it.

1. Capture

Four official pages were read on 2026-11-14 and transcribed into data/. Nothing was paraphrased into a different unit, and nothing was normalised to a "nice" number.

  • Division standings — all 32 clubs
  • League standings — cross-check of the same clubs
  • Week 10 injury report — 345 rows, 28 clubs
  • Week 10 schedule — 14 games
  • Tie-breaking procedures — the half-win tie rule

2. Verify

Checks run on every build by scripts/check_site.py:

  • Arithmetic. PF − PA = Net for all 32 clubs.
  • Completeness. 32 clubs, 8 divisions × 4, 14 games, 28 clubs on the slate.
  • Cross-page. Club records on the injury page match the standings snapshot.
  • Sources. Every dataset row carries an absolute https URL.
  • Links. Every internal href resolves to a built file; every external href is non-empty and https.
  • Recompute. The line engine, luck model and correlation engine are re-run and must reproduce the committed numbers.

3. Model

Ratings, expected scores and uncertainty are described on Our Lines. Every constant is declared there. No parameter was chosen by looking at a result.

4. What is deliberately missing

No market price. No player-level statistics. No depth-chart ordinal. No weather. No rest days. Each of those gaps is a row on Decisions and a suggestion on Suggestions. We did not fill them in.

5. Reproduce it

python3 scripts/compute_lines.py
python3 scripts/compute_luck.py
python3 scripts/compute_correlation.py
python3 scripts/build_site.py
python3 scripts/check_site.py

Any number that changes when you run those commands is a bug, not an opinion.

6. Known limits of the evidence

LimitConsequence
Eight or nine games per clubEvery rating is provisional; the bands widen on purpose.
League aggregates onlyNo opponent adjustment. A club that has played strong teams looks worse than it is.
One captured weekNo calibration, no backtest, no leaderboard.
No priceNo edge, no closing-line value, no top-tier strategies.
