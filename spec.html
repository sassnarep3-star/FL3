Blank by design — the six gaps that are visible rather than filled

A blank cell with a stated reason is a finding. A plausible number in the same cell would be a fabrication. These are the six places the project chose the first.

FieldStateWhy it is blank and what would clear it
Market price (spread / total / moneyline)blank — no licensed feed; policy PD-01Every market_* column is empty by decision, not oversight. See the policy note below.
Week 11 injury designationsblank — not published at capturenfl.com/injuries/league/2026/reg11 serves the Week 10 table; all 13 Week 11 game centres say the report is not available. No value is invented.
Week 11 depth chartblank — no capture for this weekThe Week 10 Ourlads transcription is the only depth capture held.
Snap countsblank — not published in a Game BookPlaying time would come from an official participation report; none is held. Replacement depth is therefore measured from depth-chart position, not snaps.
LAC@BAL Week 10 finalblank — game not final at captureQ2 01:05 at capture. Excluded from the corpus rather than guessed.
Week 11 weather / roofblank — kickoff more than 48h awayWeather and roof affect totals; they are only fetched inside the game-day window.

Rule of traceability

  1. Every published number names the file it is stored in and the URL of the page that published it. Those two things travel together; a number without both is not rendered.
  2. A value the source prints with an oddity (a misspelt name, a stadium called two different things, a player both inactive and in the stats) is published exactly as printed and the oddity is flagged next to it.
  3. A derived number states its formula and the inputs it used, in the same row or the same paragraph. The origination formula is validated against the 496 frozen example forecasts in data/preregistration_week11.json on every build; a deviation above 0.01 points fails the build.
  4. Any market number this project does not hold is blank. It is never entered as zero, never entered as an average of numbers we do hold, and never described as "approximately".

Older pages still say 148 games

The verified-results and verification subsites, and the executive summary, were written against a 148-game corpus before the Week 10 PIT@CIN result was final. They are owned by other sessions and were not rewritten here. Where they say 148, read 149; the reconciliation on the results page is the current number and the only place the corpus count is proved. A cross-scope update request is logged in COORDINATION.md rather than made silently.

What each new file changes about the old claims

  • The results corpus is 149 games, not 136 and not 148. See the reconciliation: 32 of 32 clubs now match the official standings exactly on W, L, T, PF and PA, and the league's points-for sum equals its points-against sum.
  • "Out" is not the same as "did not play". Across the 30 depth-1 players the official report ruled Out, 30 sat — but across the 7 the report did not rule out at all, the Game Book shows nobody started and the outcome is simply unobservable from a submitted report. The empirical table is on Availability.
  • Adding personnel to the model did not help on the only week it could be tested (13 games, all three intervals cross zero). That is published as the result on the lab page, not buried.
  • No strategy clears the demotion gate. The best Brier skill is +0.13 with a 2.5% cluster bound of −0.04, and all four Holm-adjusted p-values are above 0.05. Every strategy is demoted to paper.
