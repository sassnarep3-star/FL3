Why the market columns are blank

Policy PD-01 was decided this session and is written into data/official/results_feed.json. Its substance:

  • No licensed, timestamped price feed exists in this repository. Publishing a market number we do not hold would be an invented number wearing a source link.
  • So the project does not report ROI, expected value or closing-line value anywhere. It reports proper scores that need only an outcome — Brier, log loss, and line error against the official final margin and total.
  • The comparison the brief asks for (fair line vs market line vs difference) is therefore published as fair line and blank, with the blank labelled. The third column appears the day a licensed feed does.
  • This is a decision, not a deferral. It is written down so it cannot silently change.

Markets are originated side first, then total, then moneyline

Each snapshot stores market_order: ["side", "total", "moneyline"] and the store refuses to write a later market before an earlier one for the same fixture. The order matters because the total is informed by the side: a team's expected points and its opponent's are not independent, so deriving a total first forces an assumption about pace that the side has already told you.
