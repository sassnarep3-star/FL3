Recommended discovery sequence

1 · Establish scope

Set target markets, seasons, output audience, and paper-only boundaries. Keep the initial output research-oriented.

2 · Clear rights & data

Inventory official data, historical coverage, timestamps, corrections, access method and reuse permissions before building ingestion.

3 · Lock test design

Pre-register candidate signals, baselines, splits, costs, scoring and gates; then acquire the required odds and joint-event history.
