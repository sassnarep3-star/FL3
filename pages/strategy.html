Originating a line: proposed order

  1. Define a target and settlement convention before modeling.
  2. Build a distribution from point-in-time data, not a single outcome guess.
  3. Calibrate predicted probabilities on held-out data; compare to a simple market / historical baseline.
  4. Convert price to implied probability and remove the overround consistently; keep the raw quote and timestamp.
  5. Record projection, market quote, confidence interval, model version, and decision reason before the event.
  6. Evaluate after settlement, including pushes, voids, price availability, and costs.

Correlation register — values currently blank

There are no observations in this repository from which to compute correlation. Never substitute a guessed number or a narrative claim for measured, scoped data.

r(X,Y) = cov(X,Y) / (sd(X) × sd(Y))

For binary prop results this is a Pearson / phi correlation on paired same-game outcomes. Store sample size, season range, pairing rule, confidence interval, and feature definition alongside every value. A correlation estimate is descriptive, not proof of causal effect or stable dependence.

Origination methodology addenda (integration session · Nov 14, 2026)

These addenda extend the proposals above with the concrete research pipeline that the verified Nov 14 snapshots now make possible. They remain method documentation, not results: no signal has been tested, no price has been scored against a market, and no correlation value has been estimated.

1 · Originate our own line — the pipeline

Observe availability (point-in-time injury/depth snapshots) → build a probability distribution for each market from properly sourced data (start: points-for/against from verified standings; extend per S-04) → convert the distribution into a fair price (moneyline, spread, total; then props) → only after a licensed odds archive exists (S-05), compare our price to the market to compute edge. Until then the output is labeled a fair-value estimate, never an edge claim. Detailed write-up: docs/strategy/line-origination.md.

2 · Signal families to research (each requires pre-registration)

  • ◉ Availability/depth signals — verified report statuses and (once S-03 lands) verified backup quality; the Nov 14 snapshot supplies the first usable features.
  • ⌁ Fundamentals — points for/against differentials, home/road splits (already verified in the standings snapshot).
  • ? Situational — rest, travel, international games (the Munich game in the Week 10 schedule is the first verifiable case), bye-week effects.
  • ✓ Every candidate signal is registered with features frozen before kickoff, then tested on chronological holdouts against the published baselines.

3 · Removing luck from the verdict

Luck cannot be deleted, only bounded: fixed pre-declared sample sizes, confidence intervals from NIST-style guidance, calibration checks, comparisons against naive baselines, and replication on untouched weeks. A winning streak alone is never evidence; the leaderboard publishes uncertainty with every rank. Write-up: docs/strategy/signals-and-validation.md.

4 · Correlation for parlays and prop combos

Combination bets (parlays, player-prop combos, same-game combos) will only be priced after joint outcomes are measured from paired historical data; the dependence structure is estimated with uncertainty and stress-tested, never narrated. Until that dataset exists, combos stay out of scope for ranking (suggestion S-11). Write-up: docs/strategy/leaderboard-and-governance.md.

5 · Leaderboard competition & rewards

Strategies compete on a paper-trading leaderboard with pre-declared scoring, eligibility, sample minimums, and published uncertainty; rewards stay unspecified pending the governance review (F-07 / A-08). Rules are versioned in the repository before the competition starts and cannot change mid-run.
