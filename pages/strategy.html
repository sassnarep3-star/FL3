Strategy lab

Candidate hypotheses only. No model has been trained or evaluated because verified, timestamped feature and odds histories are not connected. Each idea must compete against a baseline and pass the validation gates before leaderboard eligibility.

Important: There is no method that removes luck from individual outcomes. The aim is to estimate whether skill is repeatable under uncertainty, not to promise a win or a “sure thing.” All strategy descriptions below are proposals, not established edges.

Originating a line: proposed order

  1. Define a target and settlement convention before modeling.
  2. Build a distribution from point-in-time data, not a single outcome guess.
  3. Calibrate predicted probabilities on held-out data; compare to a simple market / historical baseline.
  4. Convert price to implied probability and remove the overround consistently; keep the raw quote and timestamp.
  5. Record projection, market quote, confidence interval, model version, and decision reason before the event.
  6. Evaluate after settlement, including pushes, voids, price availability, and costs.

Correlation register — values currently blank

There are no observations in this repository from which to compute correlation. Never substitute a guessed number or a narrative claim for measured, scoped data.

r(X,Y) = cov(X,Y) / (sd(X) × sd(Y))

For binary prop results this is a Pearson / phi correlation on paired same-game outcomes. Store sample size, season range, pairing rule, confidence interval, and feature definition alongside every value. A correlation estimate is descriptive, not proof of causal effect or stable dependence.
