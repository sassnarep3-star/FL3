Availability & depth

Intended to identify whether an offense or defense is relying on a starter, first backup, or deeper replacement. At present, this repository has no player-level current feed or official team depth-chart snapshots; therefore no names, status, depth positions, or injury impact scores are populated.

Do not treat an injury report as a depth chart. NFL reports show practice participation and game status for listed players. They do not, by themselves, establish a player’s ordinal backup rank, snap share, replacement, or expected performance impact. Capture the source, timestamp, effective game, and roster context separately.
