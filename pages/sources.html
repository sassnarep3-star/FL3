Official source register

These are primary-source entry points checked during this repository review. Rows link to the official publisher. Dynamic NFL pages are useful for manual review but must be captured with query, season, week, and observation time before being treated as historical evidence.

No rows of player/game/stat values were imported into the site. The tables below catalogue data sources and their limits, not current betting signals. External web pages may update after this review; preserve dated copies for reproducible work.
