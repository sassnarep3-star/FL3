Validation gates

Every candidate passes all required gates before a paper-leaderboard result is eligible. These gates reduce avoidable bias; they cannot make a noisy sports outcome luck-free. Gate outcomes remain “not evaluated” until evidence exists.

Current status: No strategy has passed validation. No verified backtest rows, archived odds, or model artifacts exist in the repository. This page defines the review protocol; it reports no performance result.

Leaderboard metrics (report together)

Rank only after gate clearance. Do not rank by raw win percentage alone. Publish sample count, mean net return / ROI definition, uncertainty interval, calibration, odds / market coverage, maximum drawdown, losing streaks, and results by season / bet family. Freeze tie-breaks and minimum sample criteria before the contest; determine sample thresholds through a prospective power analysis rather than selecting them after results.

When to reject a candidate

Reject or return to research if the apparent edge disappears on the untouched chronological holdout, is concentrated in one season or narrow subgroup, is not calibrated, relies on unavailable prices, or cannot be independently reproduced. A promising result remains uncertain and needs continuing monitoring.
