How to read this. Each brief requirement (B01-B69) maps to one or more register rows. The binding state is the least-advanced state among those rows - it is what stands between the requirement and a decision. 69 of 69 brief requirements are mapped; the audit build fails if any bullet is unmapped, so a coverage gap cannot be silent. Register rows no brief bullet maps to are listed as register-internal additions at the bottom.
