149 verified games, and a corpus that reconciles to the league's own standings

The brief asks for a verified results feed that makes backtests and ROI possible. The first job is arithmetic: does the games log add up to what the league says each club has done? For 148 transcribed games it did not — two clubs were wrong. Here is the correction, the proof, and the one game that is deliberately excluded.

The correction: one missing game, exactly

Aggregating the 148-game log gave CIN 6-2, 240 PF / 175 PA and PIT 6-2, 180 PF / 144 PA. The official standings give CIN 7-2, 278 / 193 and PIT 6-3, 198 / 182. The differences are identical and opposite: one CIN win by 38-18 and one PIT loss by 18-38. That is not a rounding issue or a tie-handling issue; it is a single game, PIT@CIN in Week 10, which was not final when the log was first transcribed and was therefore correctly omitted at the time.

Adding it makes all 32 clubs match on W, L, T, PF and PA, and makes the league's points-for sum equal its points-against sum (6880). The feed now states the count: 149 games.

The brief's figure of 136 games is not reproducible from any official page. It was recorded as a corrected number rather than treated as a defect to argue about. Weeks 1-9 hold exactly 136 games; the brief is nine weeks of a ten-week season.

What the feed refuses to contain

  • A game without an official final score. LAC@BAL was still in the second quarter at capture (17-7) and is excluded from every count, every rating and every backtest.
  • A score derived from a live play-by-play page. A live page is not a result.
  • A "close enough" match. The comparison is exact on five integers per club.
