Method

Identical originated-line model run twice on the same Week 10 holdout. Ratings are calibrated on verified Weeks 1-9 only (chronological). Variant A applies the capped availability adjustment from the official Week 10 injury report; Variant B zeroes it. The two variants differ ONLY in the availability adjustment. Scored against verified Week 10 results. MAE/RMSE for spread and total; Brier for home win probability. 95% CIs are non-parametric bootstraps over the holdout games (seed 20261117, 20000 reps).

Exclusions

  • Depth-tier features are NOT modelled: the depth-tier source is unverified for most clubs (R042 BLOCKED), so the brief's gate forbids building that feature.
  • No market price is used; no bet is recommended. Model output only.
  • These are model-evaluation numbers, NOT leaderboard entries (the brief forbids backtest numbers on the leaderboard).

Limitations

  • Availability is captured for Week 10 only, so the head-to-head comparison is on the Week 10 holdout (n=12). The CIs are correspondingly wide.
  • The holdout has 12 scored Week 10 games; the full 14-game slate is not yet fully scored in the results corpus.

Sources

  • Week 10 results: NFL.com Week 10 schedule - file data/week_results_2026.csv
  • Availability (injury game status): NFL.com injury report - file data/injuries_week10.json
  • Ratings: NFL.com standings
