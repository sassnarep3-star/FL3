Flagged for Review

Every irregularity, open question and unverified item, with the link to check it. 19 open. Nothing on this page blocks the site: it is the list you asked to review after publication.

How these were found. Each item came from a direct contradiction between two official pages, an empty official page, or a request (2nd/3rd/4th string, market edge) that no official source can answer. None of them were filled in by inference.

F01 Standings page excludes a game the schedule page marks FINAL

medium severity open
Observed
nfl.com/schedules/2026/REG/ shows 'Commanders 24, Giants 21, FINAL' for the Thursday Week 10 game. nfl.com/standings/division/2026/REG still lists WAS 3-5-1 and NYG 5-3-1, i.e. before that result.
Why it matters
A line originated from the standings page is one game stale for those two clubs. Every other club is unaffected.
Could not confirm
Whether the standings page updates on a delay or simply had not refreshed at capture time. No explicit 'as of' timestamp is published on the page.
Effect on the lines
WAS@NYG has already been played, so the originated line for that game is not actionable and is shown for completeness only.
Action needed
Adopt the SG-02 freeze job so lines are originated from a kickoff-time snapshot, not a morning snapshot.
  • Schedule (shows the final score)
  • Standings (does not reflect it)

F02 A starting QB absence is missing from the official injury table

high severity open
Observed
The Week 10 injury page's own news module carries 'Ravens confirm Lamar Jackson expected to miss 6-10 weeks'. The Ravens table on the same page lists eight players and no quarterback.
Why it matters
An engine that reads only the injury table would conclude Baltimore has no QB availability problem. That is exactly the failure mode the availability signal is supposed to catch.
Could not confirm
Whether Jackson had been placed on injured reserve or otherwise removed from the report by the time the table was rendered. The table is the only official machine-readable list.
Effect on the lines
The LAC@BAL line is originated without a QB penalty for Baltimore. It should be read as too generous to BAL.
Action needed
Add a news-headline cross-check (S03 extension) or accept that the availability signal has known blind spots.

  • News article
