Leaderboard Competition — design

The contest rewards strategies (not one-off bets) that demonstrate skill after validation. Ranking uses the SkillIndex (z vs luck + CLV bonus) and its 95% lower bound — see Methodology §4. Two lists are maintained: an Official list (all gates passed) and a Provisional list (small samples, badged loudly) — per suggestion S6 option C, pending approval.

How luck is kept out of the ranking: (1) expected performance is computed at real placed prices — a lucky win pays less z-score than an unlucky loss costs; (2) CLV measures whether the price beaten was actually good; (3) the FDR-corrected skill test demotes strategies that are just the lucky tail of many entries; (4) rank-by-lower-bound means a 10-2 record with a tiny sample cannot outrank a 46-34 record with a large one.
