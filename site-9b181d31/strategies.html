Why almost everything is "blocked/pending": the brief demands luck be removed and only skill be rewarded. That is impossible with zero graded bets and unmeasured correlations — so the catalog honestly reports the gaps instead of fabricating performance. G0 (pre-registration) and G1 (provenance) are the only gates satisfiable today: both are marked accordingly.

Pre-registration block (G0) — how a strategy enters

  • Declare: ID, class, exact trigger rules, price requirement (min edge), stake rule, and stop conditions — committed to git before kickoff.
  • Grade: every trigger bet logged with placed line, closing line, result, and source links (G1).
  • Promote: gates G2–G8 run automatically as data accumulates; pass/fail recorded on the Leaderboard.
  • Retire: any strategy failing G3 (holdout) or G5 (FDR) after its sample matures is retired with a public failure record — failures stay visible to keep the competition honest.
