Requirements Register

A decision is "reasonable" only when every question it depends on is answered from verified information. This page separates what we have, what we don't have, and the specific questions that must be answered — with a discovery route for every gap. Status meanings: answered verified now · partial some data, not enough · blocked nothing verified yet.

A · Information matrix

What we already have (verified, source-linked)

  • 2026 standings, all 32 teams, through TNF Week 10 — W-L-T, PF/PA, streaks (NFL.com)
  • Complete Week 10 & Week 11 schedules incl. international games & byes (NFL.com)
  • Official Week 10 game-status injury report — every OUT/DOUBTFUL/QUESTIONABLE row (160 rows)
  • Club depth charts with replacement tiers for 7 teams (MIN, GB, SEA, DET, NE, AZ, DAL)
  • Market spreads/totals for every Week 10 game from 5 outlets (trusted tier, linked)
  • Team-level correlations & Pythagorean fit computed from the above
  • Explicit starter/backup news where sources state it (Beck starting, Watson starting, Cousins trade, Lock = QB2)

What we do NOT have (blank + flagged by policy)

  • Game-level scores/results for Weeks 1–9 (needed for SRS ratings & outcome correlations)
  • Player-level stats (needed for every player-prop model)
  • Closing-line history / odds feeds (needed for the CLV gate and line-move signals)
  • Depth charts for 24 teams — incl. HOU (published as image) and SF (page HTTP 500)
  • IR / transaction feeds (weekly reports omit IR'd and traded-away starters — BAL QB case, F16)
  • Multi-week injury trends & practice-participation history (only Wednesday snapshot + final statuses)
  • Weather, referee, and snap-count data
  • Owner parameters: bankroll, unit size, reward pool, jurisdiction/legal constraints
  • Any graded strategy results (season just mid-way; system launched this week)

B · Question register

Each question lists: what we know, what's missing, why it blocks a decision, and how to discover the answer (suggestion ID on the Suggestions Board).

Q1 · What bankroll, unit size, and loss limits govern staking?

Status: blocked — owner input required · Blocks: all position sizing, leaderboard staking rules

Know: nothing — no parameters were provided. Missing: total bankroll, unit definition (%), max exposure per week, max parlay legs, stop-loss policy. Why it blocks: without it, no bet sizing rule can be specified and the leaderboard cannot compare strategies fairly (a 100-unit strategy and a 1-unit strategy are not comparable). Discover via: owner decision (S7 option A provides a conservative default framework to approve or edit).

Q2 · What rewards does the leaderboard pay, and from what budget?

Status: blocked — owner input required · Blocks: rewards finalization, contest promotion

Know: the brief requires "rewards for top performing strategies." Missing: cash vs recognition, prize pool, payout split, season vs weekly cadence. Why it blocks: reward structure changes incentives (e.g., weekly prizes favor variance; season prizes favor skill) — the scoring formula must match. Discover via: S7 (three reward models laid out for selection).

Q3 · Which source tiers count as "official/verified" for this project?

Status: answered provisionally · Blocks: definition of verified across all tables

Know: this build treats NFL.com and official club sites (vikings.com etc.) as official, and ESPN/CBS/SBR/betus/BetMGM as trusted market/media (always labeled + linked, never "official"). Missing: owner confirmation that this tiering is acceptable. Why it blocks: market lines can never come from NFL.com; if trusted-tier data is disallowed entirely, the lines page loses its comparison column. Discover via: owner sign-off (documented on the Sources page).

Q4 · How should "starter vs 2nd/3rd/4th string" be defined when club charts disagree with reports?

Status: partial · Blocks: deficiency scoring accuracy

Know: two chart-vs-report mismatches this week (Parsons absent from GB chart, Kerby Joseph absent from DET chart); several charts self-label "unofficial" and one (MIN) lists a just-traded player 4th. Missing: a tie-break policy (e.g., report status wins; chart wins; coach statement wins). Why it blocks: the deficiency score weights depend on the tier. Discover via: S4 (tie-break policy options).

Q5 · What is the closing line, and where does line history come from?

Status: blocked — data source needed · Blocks: validation gate G4 (CLV), line-move signals

Know: current-week snapshots from 5 outlets (they disagree by up to 3 points). Missing: time-stamped odds history and official closing lines. Why it blocks: CLV is the cleanest luck-free skill measure for betting strategies; without it gate G4 cannot run. Discover via: S3 (odds-feed options).

Q6 · When will game-level results (Weeks 1–9) be ingested?

Status: blocked — data source needed · Blocks: SRS ratings, parlay-leg correlation values, backtesting

Know: aggregates only (PF/PA per team). Missing: every game score + line result. Why it blocks: outcome correlations (e.g., two favorites in one parlay) and any backtest require game rows; the correlation matrix currently has blank cells for these. Discover via: S1 (game-data ingestion options — an open GitHub-hosted dataset (nflverse) is fetchable within this environment).

Q7 · What player-stat source will power prop strategies?

Status: blocked — data source needed · Blocks: all player-prop combo models

Know: injury/depth only. Missing: targets, snaps, air yards, usage rates. Why it blocks: prop lines cannot be originated without player baselines; the prop-correlation cells stay blank. Discover via: S2.

Q8 · What counts as a "skill-based" pass — statistical threshold?

Status: partial — proposal exists · Blocks: leaderboard ranking method

Know: the proposed framework (binomial/Bootstrap test vs expected win rate at market price, FDR-corrected for multiple strategies, minimum CLV) is documented on the Methodology page. Missing: owner approval of the thresholds (e.g., p<0.05 after correction, min 50 graded bets). Discover via: owner sign-off on S6.

Q9 · Which jurisdictions / legal constraints apply to the participants?

Status: blocked — owner input required · Blocks: whether the contest may involve real stakes

Know: nothing. Missing: participant locations, whether entries are paper-tracked or real-money. Why it blocks: determines if the leaderboard is a pure forecasting contest (safe default) or involves wagering. Discover via: owner decision (S7 includes a "paper-only" default).

Q10 · Are the two anomalous market "opened" totals real moves or source errors?

Status: flagged — unverified · Blocks: confidence in open-to-close move signals

Know: ESPN shows MIA-IND total "opened 47.5" vs 38.5 now (9-pt move) and LAR-AZ "opened 47.5" vs 39.5. Missing: second-source confirmation of opening numbers. Why it blocks: open-to-close moves are a signal class; if the opens are typos the signal is noise. Discover via: S3 (multi-book history) — currently listed on Flagged as F6.

Q11 · What replaces a chart-invisible player (HOU chart is an image; SF page errors)?

Why it blocks: the user requirement to know "which string replaces a starter" cannot be answered for these teams without inference, which policy forbids. Discover via: S2/S5 (alternative chart sources or transcription workflow).

Q12 · How are strategy entries submitted and time-stamped to prevent look-ahead cheating?

Status: partial — proposal exists · Blocks: contest integrity

Know: the proposed design (entries are git-committed before kickoff; results graded afterwards). Missing: owner approval of workflow. Discover via: S6.

Q13 · Should model v0.1's large edges (SEA-LV +9.5) be treated as signals or errors?

Status: partial · Blocks: any use of v0.1 numbers operationally

Know: v0.1 is aggregate-only with design-weight injuries — known to over-adjust vs market. Missing: validation history. Why it blocks: acting on a 9.5-pt edge from an unvalidated model is a luck-based decision. Discover via: gate G3 out-of-sample test once S1 data lands; until then edges are research-only (explicitly stated on Lines).
