Decisions

Everything that has to be answered before a decision can be called reasonable. 9 of 13 questions are blocking: while they are open, no strategy in their family can be judged good or bad.

Questions, knowledge and gaps

IDFamilyQuestionWhat we know (verified)What we do not knowBlocks?What discovers it
Q01Data foundationWhich official NFL source is the single source of truth for each field, and what is its refresh cadence?Standings, injuries, schedule and news headlines are all available on nfl.com. Standings and injuries are snapshotted for 2026-11-14.Whether nfl.com publishes a stable machine-readable feed, or whether the HTML must be scraped. No feed was found and none was probed beyond the public pages.blockingSuggestion SG-01 (source-contract review) — record accept header, cadence and failure mode for each page.
Q02Data foundationIs 'as of this morning' the right snapshot semantics, or do we need as-of-kickoff?The standings page currently disagrees with the schedule page about WAS/NYG (flag F01).How often that lag appears, and whether it also affects PF/PA mid-week.blockingSuggestion SG-02 (freeze-the-line job) — diff the two pages weekly and record every disagreement.
Q03Injuries and availabilityWhat exactly do 'Did Not Participate', 'Limited' and 'Full' imply for the probability a player suits up?The three practice statuses exist and are printed per player. Game status (Out/Doubtful/Questionable) is printed separately.The league's own stated mapping from practice status to game availability. The official policy page returned an empty body (flag F06). We therefore apply a declared weight (Out 1.00, Doubtful 0.60, Questionable 0.25) that is a model choice, not a league rule.blockingSuggestion SG-03 (policy capture) — re-fetch the policy page via a rendering client and archive a dated copy.
Q04Injuries and availabilityWhen a listed player has no game status, is he expected to play?185 of 345 Week 10 rows have a blank game status (flag F04).Whether blank means 'expected to play' or 'not yet declared'. The official table does not say.blockingSuggestion SG-04 (inactives reconciliation) — compare the 90-minute inactive list to the Friday report and count how often a blank-status player is inactive.
Q05Injuries and availabilityHow do we identify 2nd/3rd/4th string without an official depth chart?No official NFL source publishes a machine-checkable depth ordinal (flag F05).Everything downstream: replacement-level drop-off, which specific backup starts, and the true size of an availability penalty.blockingSuggestion SG-05 (snap-count evidence) — use official participation counts (if published) as the ordinal proxy instead of a depth chart.
Q06LinesWhat is our line compared to, if we hold no licensed market price?Our own originated lines exist for all 14 Week 10 games, with bands.Whether those lines are better or worse than the market. Unknown by definition without a price (flag F07).blockingSuggestion SG-06 (legal price capture) — either license a feed or accept that 'edge vs market' can never be claimed.
Q07LinesHow wide should the line be when a club has played eight games?The engine widens sigma by sqrt(1 + 8/games_played).Whether that widening is calibrated. Nothing has been graded yet, so sigma is a declared constant.non-blockingSuggestion SG-07 (calibration backtest) — needs historical week snapshots, which do not exist yet.
Q08StrategiesWhat counts as a passing grade, given that zero bets have been graded?The tier ladder and gate definitions exist and are enforced by scripts.Any real hit rate, edge, or closing-line value.non-blockingSuggestion SG-08 (grade-the-gates dry run) — simulate grading on Week 10 once results are final to prove the pipeline works end to end.
Q09StrategiesDoes the luck filter actually remove luck, or just rename it?Pythagorean luck is measured for all 32 clubs; range +/- 2.17 wins.Whether fading lucky clubs is profitable. That is an empirical claim and the data to test it does not exist here.blockingSuggestion SG-09 (luck backtest) — needs a multi-season game-level history with closing lines.
Q10CorrelationHow big is the same-game correlation between prop legs?Direction is certain for COR-A2/A3/A4 from mechanism. Cross-game legs are structurally independent (COR-A5).Every magnitude. Player-level official stats were not captured.blockingSuggestion SG-10 (player-game stats capture) — unlocks all prop correlation work.
Q11GovernanceWho is allowed to change a published line after kickoff?Nothing is protected beyond git history.Whether the audit trail is sufficient without a signed tag per week.non-blockingSuggestion SG-11 (signed weekly snapshots) — tag each week's line file at first kickoff.
Q12GovernanceWhat is the reward actually rewarding — accuracy, or honesty about uncertainty?Score uses sqrt(n) normalisation plus a luck penalty.Whether a strategy that abstains a lot but wins when it acts should outrank a high-volume, small-edge strategy. The current formula says yes; that is a choice, not a fact.non-blockingSuggestion SG-12 (reward-scheme A/B) — run both weightings on the same graded set once one exists.
Q13GovernanceWhat is the reward, and who is allowed to receive it?A scoring formula and five tiers exist. Rewards are described only as ranking credit.Prize type, eligibility, jurisdiction, tax treatment, appeal path. None designed.blockingSuggestion SG-12 plus flag F12 — decide reward scope before any tier-3 score is paid.
