Correlation

Correlation decides whether a parlay is genuinely two bets or the same bet twice. The measured rows below are computed from the verified standings by scripts/compute_correlation.py; the assumed rows say plainly that the size is unknown.

The single most useful number here is r = −0.204. Across 32 clubs, how much a team scores and how much it allows are close to independent. That kills the intuition that "good offence plus bad defence" is a natural hedge.

Declared assumptions (magnitude unverified)

Direction comes from mechanism and is not in doubt. Size is not measured, because player-level official statistics were not captured.

COR-A1Same-game: home spread and totalunmeasurednear zero, direction unverifiedA blowout can come from a fast start (raises total) or a shutdown (lowers it). Margin and total have no monotone link.Treat as independent for now, but do not let one leg exceed 1/2 of the parlay weight.
COR-A2Same-game: QB passing yards + team total pointsunmeasuredpositive, magnitude unverifiedPassing volume drives scoring, but a trailing team passes more while scoring is already set, and a leading team runs out the clock.Positive correlation inflates the joint probability; any pricing that treats the legs independently will overstate the payout edge.
COR-A3Same-game: RB rushing yards + team spread (team winning)unmeasuredpositive, magnitude unverifiedLeading teams run more; a run-heavy script is a consequence of leading, which is the same event as covering.This is the strongest known correlation family in NFL props. Do not stack RB rushing overs with opponent-spread legs.
COR-A4Same-game: WR receiving yards + QB passing yardsunmeasuredstrongly positive, magnitude unverifiedShared targets. If the WR hits, the QB very likely hit.Stacking these two as independent legs is close to betting the same event twice. Gate PARLAY-IND requires an explicit shared-driver declaration.
COR-A5Cross-game parlay legs (different games, different clubs)+0.00independent by constructionNo shared players, no shared clock, no shared weather. The only shared term is league-wide scoring environment, which is a level, not an outcome.Multipliers are still cut, because a shared league-wide shock (e.g. a scoring boom) hits every over simultaneously.
COR-A6Availability-driven change in the same game (starter out -> total and spread)unmeasureddirectional, sign depends on side of the ballA missing starter on offence lowers that club's scoring; on defence it raises the opponent's scoring. Both move the spread the same way; the total moves in OPPOSITE directions.Do not treat 'player out' as automatically 'under'. This registry exists specifically to block that heuristic.

Parlay rules

RuleWhatWhy
PR-1Never price a parlay by multiplying legs unless every shared driver is listed.r(A4) between QB and WR yardage legs is close to 1 in mechanism, so naive multiplication exaggerates the payout.
PR-2Cap same-game legs at 2 per ticket in v0.Every additional same-game leg adds an unmeasured correlation term. Two legs is the most we can decompose with the evidence we hold.
PR-3Apply a haircut equal to the largest unmeasured correlation term in the ticket.Conservative pricing. The haircut is shown on the leaderboard so the cost of uncertainty is visible rather than hidden.
PR-4Cross-game legs must come from games played on different days OR different clubs; same-club, different-game is impossible by definition.Structural independence (COR-A5) only holds if no player or club is shared.

What cannot be measured right now

  • Any correlation involving a market price — No licensed official odds feed (flag F07). Correlations between our line and the market's line cannot be computed because we do not hold the market's line.
  • Player-level prop correlations — Official per-game player statistics were not captured in this build. COR-A2/A3/A4 remain assumptions with magnitude unverified.
