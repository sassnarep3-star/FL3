THE DECISION

Do not publish a “best bet” yet.

The repository started as a five-byte README with no application, verified dataset, odds feed, injury pipeline, or test history. This site establishes a sourced research plan and review queue; it does not claim tested profitability.

  • ✓ Official NFL injury, stats, rules, and Next Gen Stats entry points catalogued.
  • ✓ Proposed single, parlay, and player-prop research paths separated.
  • ! No performance, player status, line, depth rank, or correlation value is populated without a dated source dataset.

EXECUTIVE TAKEAWAYREAD FIRST

Skill can be tested.
Luck cannot be deleted.

Use pre-registered hypotheses, point-in-time data, out-of-sample evaluation, calibration, and uncertainty intervals. A positive record alone is not proof of repeatable edge.

Recommended next actionResolve data rights, odds provider, and time-stamped historical coverage before building live projections.
