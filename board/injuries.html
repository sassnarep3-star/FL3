Week 10 availability - injuries, backups and what they cost a team

Source: the official NFL injury report for Week 10 (SRC-001), read 2026-11-14T03:08Z–03:16Z. All 28 clubs in the Week 10 slate were read (the page renders in 8 chunks; chunks 0-6 carry the club tables), but only 26 of them contribute a row with a game status: Baltimore's section carries practice designations with no Out/Doubtful/Questionable value, and the Chargers' section contributes none — and those two clubs play each other on Monday night, so their availability_flags on Lines are blank because nothing was published, not because anyone is healthy (FLG-028). The Monday and Thursday slates are included.

What the table below is

Every player on the Week 10 report who carries a game status (Out / Doubtful / Questionable), with the injury text, the final practice participation and the game they belong to. 160 rows. Rows with only a practice designation (no game status) are counted per club in the availability-load table below, not listed individually, because the point of this page is what a lineup looks like after the designations land.

position_group is an internal mapping from the report's position string (T/G/C → line, DE/DT → front, CB/S → secondary, etc.) and is labelled as such - the report's own position field is not always right (Seahawks QB Patrick O'Connell appears as LB, FLG-007).

What the counts show for the Sunday slate: Minnesota and Seattle each have a QB listed Out (Murray; Darnold), Green Bay is missing a starting RB and a starting edge rusher, Cleveland's report is nearly empty while Houston's is missing a pass-rusher and a top receiver, New Orleans has three offensive-line designations, and so on.

What they do not show: Baltimore's report contains eight rows and no game status at all, while the official news feed on the same page says the franchise quarterback is expected to miss 6-10 weeks (FLG-005). A player on injured reserve does not appear on the weekly report. Any deficiency metric built from the report alone will therefore call the most important absence of the week "healthy" - which is the single most important fact on this page.

Rules this page is built on

  • Filing cadence (SRC-011, official): clubs file a Practice Report by 4:00 p.m. New York time on

Wednesday, Thursday and Friday for a Sunday game; Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday for a Thursday game; Thursday/Friday/Saturday for a Monday game; and the equivalents for other days. The fetcher is scheduled to those deadlines, not to an arbitrary cron.

  • A rested player is on the report too: the policy lists non-injury absences as

Not injury related - resting player (five such rows this week), and the game status can still be Out (JAX Bates, JAX Davis, HOU Al-Shaair). "Out" is therefore not always medical.

  • Full practice + Questionable is normal (NYJ Wilson, KC Walker III, MIA Baker Jr…) and so is

full practice + Out (JAX Green II). The designation is a disclosure obligation, not a prediction (FLG-009).

  • Probable is not in the modern report. The page's own vocabulary is Out / Doubtful / Questionable

and V-10 rejects anything else; the historical probability language ("Questionable = 50%") is not part of the policy and is not used anywhere in this repo.

How a model is supposed to use this

1. designation → availability event (who is unavailable, for which game). 2. position group × unit → which phase is degraded (line, front seven, secondary, backfield). 3. missing depth chart → how degraded — this step is currently impossible from official data and is why availability_adjustment in the model register is value: null (Q-02, FLG-017). 4. practice trajectory → probability the designation converts to active, which is the input to signal SIG-02 and cannot be read from a single day's report at all - the fetcher therefore stores every filing per week, not just the last one.

Irregularities on this page, listed plainly

  • 8 clubs have a player listed on their report who, before 2026, was associated with another club

(FLG-006); all are marked FLG-012 in the row's flag column and none was silently corrected.

  • One row (NE, Hunter Henry) is Questionable with an empty injury field; the blank is published as

a blank (FLG-008).

  • The context statements on this page (Jackson 6-10 weeks, Darnold out / Lock starting, Willis to IR,

Nacua to IR, Jacobs surgery) come from headline text on the official page; the articles behind them were not opened this session, so they are inputs to the research queue, not to any price (FLG-022).

Every table on this site is generated from data/ by scripts/build_site.py; no value is typed into the HTML. Empty cells are deliberate: the value was not confirmable from an official source, so it is left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Nothing here is betting advice.
