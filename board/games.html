2026 game results - verified rows

Scores read from the league's own weekly schedule pages: Week 1 (SRC-006) and Week 2 (SRC-007), plus the completed Week 10 Thursday game from the injury/schedule page (SRC-001).

Coverage: 33 rows of the 137 games the league has played. That is a declared gap, not an omission: the remaining 104 games are readable from the same pages by the same script, and the sandbox this session ran in blocks outbound requests to nfl.com (probe recorded in COORDINATION.md), so the fetcher ships untested rather than the table shipping invented (FLG-016, FLG-024).

Why each row can be trusted

Every game row was cross-checked against the running records printed on the same page: for all 32 clubs the win/loss state after Week 1 and after Week 2 in data/standings_2026.csv matches the results below (e.g. BUF 7-1 with a 36-31 and a 41-31 win; LAC 0-8 having lost 14-26 and 14-26). V-05 in the verification log checks that every row references two of the 32 clubs.

One row-level oddity is kept exactly as published: the Week 1 opener's section header says "Sunday Night Football, September 9th" while the game line says Wednesday, September 9th (FLG-011).
