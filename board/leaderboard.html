Leaderboard, rewards and staking

The brief wants the strategies competing on a leaderboard that pays the best ones. That is a good idea with a fatal failure mode: if the reward is paid on realised profit, the rational entry is a high-variance bettor, and the prize selects for luck. Everything below is designed so that the strategy that wins the board is the strategy that is actually good at pricing.

Sixteen strategies, all no settled bets. No number on this table has been invented. That is the point: the board is a real object in the repo, its schema is the evaluation contract, and filling it is the work.

Scoring


skill_score =  w1 * (-log_loss_vs_market, z-scored)          # prediction quality  - primary
             + w2 * (mean CLV in points, z-scored)           # market-beating      - primary
             + w3 * sign(luck_adjusted_roi) * min(1, bets/N_min)   # profitability, sample-weighted
             - w4 * (max_drawdown / bankroll_limit)
             - penalty(gate breaches)                        # G-02/G-10/G-12 breaches -> demote + void
weights:  w1 = 0.35, w2 = 0.35, w3 = 0.25, w4 = 0.05

Two choices matter:

  • Prediction quality is scored on log-loss, not on win/loss. Log-loss punishes a 70% claim that

loses and rewards a well-calibrated 55% claim; it is measurable on ~200 bets where ROI needs thousands (see the decision math sample-size rows).

  • **Profit is sample-weighted, never displayed raw.** A strategy with 40 bets at +18% ROI ranks below

one with 900 bets at +4% ROI, and min(1, bets/N_min) is what encodes that.

Rewards are then allocated from skill_score, with a share cap per strategy so the board cannot be won by one oversized lucky week, and a freeze: a strategy's method hash is recorded at entry (gate G-12) and changing the model mid-season forfeits the standing. Full formula and tier rules live in models/leaderboard.yaml.

Reward design: the pool is paid 60% on the season's skill_score, 25% on the improvement in skill_score (so a new strategy can earn without an all-time record), and 15% to the best documented negative result - a prize for killing a popular signal with evidence. That last line is deliberate: a board that only pays winners produces a research culture that never publishes losses, which is exactly the failure mode the rest of this repo exists to prevent.

Staking rules (all of them, because they are short)

1. Break-even is 52.38% at −110 and 54.55% at −120 props; below that nothing compounds. 2. Fractional Kelly at 0.25 of the measured edge; never of the assumed edge. 3. Per-bet cap 2% of bankroll; per-slate cap 8%; per-causal-driver cap 3% (gate G-08's exposure rule). 4. Stand-down after two consecutive gate breaches, or any G-10 data failure. 5. A bet is void for scoring if it was placed after our published number was updated (timestamp check).

These are parameter choices, not truths - and the caps themselves await Q-17: the owner has to state the bankroll and the acceptable drawdown. Until then the tiers are the policy, and the numbers inside them are placeholders by design.
