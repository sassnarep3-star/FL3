Week 10 matchup deficiency matrix

The full interactive matrix lives on the sibling dashboard: Matchup matrix at the site root — 168 rows (14 games × 2 directions × 6 declared units), first through fourth strings on both sides, with replacement depth counted only where the official Week 10 report rules a first-string player Out.

Sources and authority

Availability: the official NFL Week 10 injury report (SRC-001), captured 2026-11-14. A player absent from the report is no designation published, never "healthy". Order: the targeted Ourlads capture data/depth_charts_2026-11-16.json (14 clubs, fetched 2026-11-16) for those clubs, and the 2026-11-14 transcription data/depth_risk.json for the other 14 playing clubs. Ourlads is a third-party scouting service; it supplies order only, never availability. Where a chart marker disagrees with the official report, the report wins and the disagreement is published on the root page. * The declared slot map (LWR/RWR/SWR → WR, LDE/RDE/RUSH → DE, …) is a display convention printed on the root page. The league publishes no depth rank; nothing here invents one.

State: PARTIAL (readiness R036)

102 of 168 rows have both sides' order evidence; the other 66 are explicit n/a rows naming the missing group, club and source. 25 rows carry a confirmed promotion (first-string ruled Out). Doubtful/Questionable first strings are shown but never counted as promotions. 8 chart-marker vs official-report conflicts are listed on the root page with both source links. Gate: official club-by-club depth order for all 32 clubs (R042) and the shared/empty-slot schema (R064/R069) are what clear the remaining n/a cells. Until then the matrix stays descriptive — it counts strings and computes no score, projection or edge.

Every table on this site is generated from data/ by scripts/build_site.py; no value is typed into the HTML. Empty cells are deliberate: the value was not confirmable from an official source, so it is left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Nothing here is betting advice.
