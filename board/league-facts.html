Why these are on a site about betting lines

FactConsequence for the system
F-01 18 regular-season weekssets the season denominator for every "N bets per year" calculation behind gate G-01
F-02 two 2026 internationals (East Melbourne W1, Munich NE-DET W10)rest/travel and venue features must treat these separately - a "days of rest" model that ignores a 15,000 km trip is wrong by construction; W10's Munich game kicks off 9:30 a.m. ET, which is why kickoff time is a stored field and not an assumption
F-03 opener printed as Wednesday 2026-09-09the schedule page's own section header says "Sunday Night Football, September 9th" while the game line says Wednesday - published as read, flagged (FLG-011)
F-04 … F-07 the 2026 rule changes (6-1-3 alignment, 6-1-5 dead-ball, 6-1-6 kicking-team encroachment, 19-2 personnel review)each one changes a countable event; a totals model that ignores a dead-ball change is silently mis-specified, so rule changes are on the annual review calendar
F-08 filing cadence (Wed/Thu/Fri 4 p.m. ET for a Sunday game; Mon/Tue/Wed for Thursday; Thu/Fri/Sat for Monday)the fetcher is scheduled to these deadlines; and the three-filing "trajectory" feature only exists for Sunday games, which is the whole of SIG-02
F-09 rested players appear on the reportOut is not always medical: 5 rows this week are "Not injury related", three of them still Out - so a naive "count the Outs" feature counts coaches' rotation decisions as injuries
F-10 standings column definitionsthe only label set we accepted without a machine-readable header, because PCT and NET PTS reconcile arithmetically (V-01, V-02)
F-11/F-13 137 games played, 45.92 combined pointsthe denominator and the anchor: our Week-10 totals average 47.1 against this, which is how we know the level is right and the dispersion is wrong
F-12 exactly 1 tied game in the standingstie/push handling is a real pricing term; the fixture itself is not in our 33-row game table (FLG-021)
F-14 the league publishes no oddsthe reason every market_* column on Lines is blank, and the reason Q-01 is the top blocking question

Verified how, exactly

verified = read directly from the cited official page this session. verified-from-snippet = the text was seen in a search snippet of the official page and the page itself could not be opened here; that is good enough to build a scheduler around and not good enough to price with. verified-with-exception and derived-verified-flagged = read from the official source, with a conflict or gap recorded on Flagged for review.

Rules we could NOT verify, and therefore did not encode

  • Overtime (Rule 16) mechanics for 2026. The 2026 amendments listed above are named on the

operations page, but the rulebook PDF itself was not parsed in this session, so the playoff-OT procedure that would change end-of-game pricing is recorded as Q-09 rather than modelled (FLG-003).

  • Roster rules (53-man, practice-squad elevations, IR return windows). The practice-squad elevation

number is disputed between sources we are not allowed to treat as official - 2 or 3 per week (FLG-004). It matters: it is the difference between "the starting RB is gone for four weeks" and "he comes back next Sunday". No elevation-dependent feature exists in this repo.

  • Injury-report obligation text. The cadence above is official; the penalty/review machinery around

non-compliance is only in secondary sources, so it is a question, not a fact.
