Injury / availability ablation (chronological holdout)

The full ablation study lives on the sibling dashboard: Ablations at the site root — the identical originated-line model is run twice on the same Week 10 holdout: once with the verified availability features (Week 10 official injury-report game statuses) and once without them. This is the brief's requirement to replace fixed injury penalties with measured ablations (readiness R028 / R029).

What was compared

Variant A — availability ON: the published model, applying the capped availability adjustment built from the official Week 10 injury report (Out / Doubtful / Questionable). Variant B — availability OFF: the identical model with the availability adjustment zeroed. * The two variants share the same power ratings (calibrated on verified Weeks 1-9 only) and differ only in the availability adjustment.

Method and uncertainty

Chronological split: ratings are fixed on verified Weeks 1-9; every forecast is scored against the verified Week 10 results played afterwards. Metrics: MAE and RMSE for the spread and the total; Brier for the home win probability. * Uncertainty: non-parametric bootstrap over the holdout games (seed 20261117, 20,000 reps), 95% percentile intervals, plus a paired difference (ON − OFF) for each metric.

Honest result

The head-to-head is on the root page. On the Week 10 holdout the availability features show no demonstrated spread-MAE or Brier advantage (both paired 95% CIs span zero). The total-MAE paired difference is negative with a CI that excludes zero, but with only the scored Week 10 games in the holdout the sample is small, so it is reported as a single-window result — no skill advantage is claimed. These are model-evaluation numbers and are not leaderboard entries; the brief forbids backtest numbers on the leaderboard.

Sources and authority

Availability: the official NFL Week 10 injury report (data/injuries_week10.json), captured 2026-11-14/16. Results: the verified per-game results corpus data/week_results_2026.csv (Weeks 1-10, every row linked to an official NFL.com schedule page). * Ratings: power ratings built from Weeks 1-9 cumulative standings reconstructed from those verified scores.

Exclusions and limitations

Depth-tier features are excluded. The depth-tier source is unverified for most clubs (readiness R042 is BLOCKED), so the brief's own gate forbids building that feature until a route is verified for all 32 clubs. Availability is captured for Week 10 only, so the head-to-head comparison is on the scored Week 10 holdout (small n; wide intervals). * No market price is used and no bet is recommended. Model output only.

Every table on this site is generated from data/ by scripts/build_site.py; no value is typed into the HTML. Empty cells are deliberate: the value was not confirmable from an official source, so it is left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Nothing here is betting advice.
