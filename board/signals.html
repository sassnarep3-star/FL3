Signal catalogue

Sixteen candidate signals, written as falsifiable specifications rather than opinions. Every signal carries: the hypothesis, the trigger that must fire from data the repo actually has, the market it points at, its sample status, its gate status, and - critically - its correlation with the other signals and with the same game's other bets.

Nothing here is "live": all 16 are blocked at gate G-01 or worse, because the inputs that would test them (market prices, full game history, lineups) do not exist in the repo yet. That is the state of the art on 2026-11-14, and pretending otherwise is the specific failure this whole project is built to avoid.

Two of them are worth reading in full

SIG-01 - starting-QB replacement. Hypothesis: the move that matters is not the designation but the identity of the replacement, and the market is slowest exactly when the replacement is a third-stringer because the report does not say who that is. Trigger today: a QB is Out on the report (this week: Kyler Murray at MIN, Sam Darnold at SEA) or an official wire says the IR designation has landed. Required input the repo does not have: the next QB up and how many career attempts he has (Q-02). Until then the direction is stated, the size is unmeasured, and no stake is computed. The correlation note matters as much: this signal is the same event as every passing-volume prop in the game, so a "three-leg parlay" built from it is one bet with extra vig.

SIG-16 - model-vs-market residual at the close. This is not really a signal; it is the scoring function for all of them. Definition: take the game only when |model − close| exceeds the abstain threshold; evaluate with mean CLV and log-loss versus the close. Every other row in this table should be promotable to "bet" only by improving SIG-16's numbers, not by its own P&L. If a signal cannot be expressed as an improvement in SIG-16, it is a narrative.

The promotion ladder

1. spec (all 16 today) - written, inputs named, no numbers. 2. backtest-eligible - inputs exist and pass gate G-10; still no money. 3. paper - runs against live data with timestamped predictions (hash-committed, gate G-12); ≥ N_min bets (gate G-01 - see Validation: a 3-point hit-rate edge at -110 needs about 2,169 bets to be shown at 95% confidence, not 30). 4. capped-live - small fixed stake, CLV tracked, limits and fill-rate logged (G-06, G-11). 5. funded - only after G-03 (CLV), G-04 (FDR), G-05 (sensitivity), G-09 (regime) all pass.

Signals are allowed to move down the ladder instantly. A gate breach (G-02 violated, G-10 failed, or a flagged input used) demotes to spec and voids the results - which is what makes the reward on the leaderboard mean something.
