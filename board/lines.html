Origination output - Week 10 slate

These are our numbers for the games still to come in Week 10, produced by the pipeline on Model and written to data/lines_origination.csv. They are not bettable prices. The status column says so on every row, and the reason is structural, not cosmetic:

1. the constants behind them are fitted on 33 games in-sample (beta 1.14, sigma 11.92); 2. there is no home-field term you could defend (alpha = -0.82 ± 2.09); 3. no schedule adjustment exists; 4. no market number exists to compare against - the market_ and gap_to_market columns are blank because no official source publishes odds (FLG-010, Q-01); 5. availability adjustment is null, so a team missing its starting QB (MIN, SEA) is priced the same as one that is healthy - note availability_flags and see Injuries. An empty* flags cell has two possible meanings and the site does not guess which: either nothing was flagged, or the club published no game status at all (Baltimore and the LA Chargers, the Monday pair - FLG-028).

How to read a row

The one played game is the only honest test available. Thursday's Washington at New York finished 24-21 (SRC-008): our model expected a home margin of +3.5 (Giants favored) on a total of 44.9. The game landed at -3 on a total of 45. One game proves nothing - the point is that the pipeline produces a falsifiable pair of numbers before kickoff, and the error is computable afterwards. That is the whole mechanism: 33 games of those pairs is what turns beta and sigma into something you can defend.

The unplayed slate shows the failure mode. Buffalo at the Jets carries model_spread_home = +15.8 (New York a 15.8-point home dog, i.e. our line is Buffalo -15.8 on the road) - a number no sensible operator would stake. The mean absolute spread across the slate is 8.6 points, and model_total ranges 29.8 to 59.5 (mean 47.1 against a verified league average of 45.92). Same diagnosis in both cases, and both are visible in the register:

The totals are level-correct (47.1 vs 45.9) and dispersion-wrong (29.8 to 59.5 is not a real range for 14 games) - an unidentified gamma on 33 games (FLG-026).

Why publishing this anyway is the right call

A dashboard that only shows numbers the pipeline is confident in would show an empty table on 2026-11-14. So the site publishes the output with its status on every row, and the abstain rule keeps actionable = no. When Q-01 and Q-04 land, this table gains close_spread, gap, ev, stake and a per-row gate_status, and becomes the screen where every bet is recorded before it is placed (gate G-12). Nothing about the page has to be redesigned - only the blanks get filled.
