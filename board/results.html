Verified results feed — Week 10, 2026

This page did not exist before 2026-11-16, and the site could not be scored without it.

Every line, rating and availability number on this site was originated from a snapshot read on 2026-11-14. Week 10's Sunday slate was played on 2026-11-15. Until this page was built the site priced twelve games that had already finished and had no record of how any of them ended. That is the difference between a model and a press release.

What is verified here, and how

Each row below is a score read from the NFL's own Week 10 schedule page on 2026-11-16 at 01:30 UTC, with the individual game page for that fixture as the per-row link. Two of the fourteen games had not finished at that clock time; they are blank and marked not-final. They are not estimated, and they are not marked as anything they are not.

Why the feed is built this way

  • One row per fixture, keyed on the same game_id the slate uses. The three line files in

this repository key games three different ways (W10-01, WAS-NYG-W10, and full club names). Joining them by name is how the errors on the lines page went unnoticed.

  • A clock time on every capture. Not a build date, not a "retrieved" note — the UTC instant

of the read, so a future scoring run can prove what was knowable and when.

  • No score is taken from a standings table. Standings are derived and lag; the schedule page

is the primary record. See F01 for the case where the two disagreed.

What this unlocks, and what it still does not

It unlocks scoring: see the removal-of-luck panel for the first prospective grades this repository has ever produced.

It does not unlock ROI. A return needs a price, and this repository holds no licensed market price (Q-01 / FLG-010). The two market quotes that do exist in data/2026-week10-lines.csv are attributed to ESPN and Sportsbook Review — third-party media, not an odds vendor, and quoting them is not a licence to compute a return on them.
