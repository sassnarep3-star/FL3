# FL3 — Executive Summary (as of November 14, 2026)

## What this project is

FL3 is a research desk for originating our own NFL lines, developing skill-based
signals, and ranking strategies on a governed leaderboard. It publishes **verified
point-in-time data snapshots** and a **methodology**, not picks. The live dashboard is at
https://sassnarep3-star.github.io/FL3/.

## What does not exist yet (honest gaps)

- **No odds history** → "edge vs market" cannot be computed; our prices are fair-value
  estimates only. Blocks: edge claims, closing-line-value measures.
- **No historical point-in-time injury archive** → only one snapshot exists (Nov 14, 2026).
  Blocks: backtests of availability signals.
- **No verified depth charts** → starter→backup (2nd/3rd/4th string) mappings stay blank.
  Blocks: replacement-strength quantification. The derived team deficiency counts
  (Out/Doubtful/Questionable by unit) are published from the verified report only.
- **No joint outcome dataset** → parlay/player-prop-combo correlations cannot be estimated.
  Blocks: combo and parlay pricing.
- **No validated strategy, no backtest, no leaderboard results** → the leaderboard is
  designed (paper-only) but empty.
- **No rewards framework** → governance/legal review required before any reward exists.

## Bottom line

The desk can now **observe** (verified snapshots), **originate** (documented fair-value
methodology), and **govern** (question, suggestion, and flag registers). It cannot yet
**measure edge** (no odds), **backtest** (no archive), **verify depth** (no depth charts),
or **price combos** (no joint outcomes). The fastest path to a decision-grade system, in
order: (1) start scheduled snapshot collection (S-01/S-02), (2) pick an odds provider
(S-05), (3) ingest official depth charts (S-03), (4) pre-register the first single-bet
strategy and its gates (S-06/S-11).
