1 · Line origination model (v0.1) model output

We originate our own lines from verified inputs only. The model is deterministic and reproducible: python3 models/originate_lines.py. Design parameters are choices, not facts — they are listed so they can be tested and tuned by the validation gates.

Inputs (verified)

  • Team scoring rates: PF/game, PA/game from NFL.com standings (32 teams, through TNF Week 10).
  • Official Week 10 game-status report: OUT / DOUBTFUL / QUESTIONABLE for every player.
  • Club depth-chart tiers (1st–4th string) where verified (7 teams); tier-unverified players use a documented default weight and carry a flag.
  • Game context: home/away, neutral site (Munich — no home field), bye weeks.

Formulas

Strength rating
R(team) = (PF − PA) / games — net points per game (regressed by 0.5 for small-sample/schedule noise)
Expected points (home)
Eh = (PFh/Gh + PAa/Ga)/2 + HFA/2 − Σ(off-injury whome) + Σ(def-injury waway) + Σ(ST w)/2
Expected points (away)
Ea = (PFa/Ga + PAh/Gh)/2 − HFA/2 − Σ(off-injury waway) + Σ(def-injury whome) + Σ(ST w)/2
Spread / total
spread(home) = round½(Eh − Ea); total = round½(Eh + Ea)
Home field (HFA)
2.0 pts at home; 0.0 at neutral sites (Munich Week 10; Mexico City Week 11)
Injury weight
w = base(position) × tier-multiplier × status-multiplier

4 · Removing luck: validation gates & skill scoring

A strategy is selectively skill-based only if its results are extremely unlikely under pure luck. Every strategy passes these gates in order; a failure stops promotion to the leaderboard.

Skill score (leaderboard ranking formula)

SkillIndex = z = (observed units − expected units at market price) / σluck, where σluck = √(Σ pi(1−pi) / n) in per-bet probability terms, plus a CLV bonus: Score = z + 0.5·t(mean CLV). Rankings use the lower bound of the 95% CI of the Score (gate G7). Strategies that fail any gate appear in the catalog with gate status but are excluded from the official ranking. This is what "selectively skill-based" means here: the leaderboard ranks evidence of skill, not outcomes.

Worked example of luck removal: a strategy going 8-2 on even-money bets has a ~5.5% chance under pure coin-flipping (binomial). That looks great — but if 40 such strategies exist, the expected number of 8-2 or better records is ~2.2. Gate G5's FDR correction is exactly what turns "looks skilled" into "demonstrably skilled."
