Two that are worth reading because they are uncomfortable, not because they are big

  • FLG-026 - the calibration returned beta = 1.14 (anti-shrinkage) and pushed the total-model exponent

to its grid boundary. The tempting response is to clamp the numbers into something that looks sane; the correct response is what happened here: publish the anomaly, and let it stand as the measurement of how little 33 games tell you.

  • FLG-024 - the fetch scripts in this repo have never been executed. They ship labelled as

untested, because the alternative (claiming an auto-refreshing data layer that was never run) is the kind of fiction that would make every other number on the site untrustworthy.

Resolution protocol

1. A flag is opened by any session in data/flags.csv; the site picks it up on the next build. 2. severity: high items that touch a published value also get a row-level marker in the data file (e.g. the flag column on [injury rows](injuries.html)), so a downstream consumer cannot miss it. 3. Resolving means the data changed, the source changed, or the owner recorded an exception. Marking a flag resolved without one of those three in the commit message is treated as a gate failure (G-12).
