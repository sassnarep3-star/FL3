Originated Lines — Week 10 model output

Our lines are produced by the documented v0.1 model (net-points ratings + HFA + transparent injury weights — full spec on Methodology, reproducible via models/originate_lines.py). Market numbers are reference snapshots from five outlets, each row linking its source. Edge = model margin − market-implied margin (positive leans toward the home side vs the market). These are research comparisons, NOT recommendations — no strategy has passed the validation gates.

Read the edges skeptically: v0.1 is unvalidated and its injury weights are design choices. An edge of 9.5 (SEA-LV) more likely signals model error or missing data than a 9.5-point market mistake. Gate G3 (out-of-sample testing) is what converts these numbers into evidence — see S1.

How the market columns were chosen

  • Each game lists a primary source (most recent unambiguous quote) and the range across books (ESPN, SBR/bet365, CBS, betus, BetMGM — all linked on Sources).
  • Where books disagree by ≥1.5 points or flip the favorite, the row carries a disagreement badge and the game appears on Flagged.
  • Market numbers are a moving snapshot (captured 2026-11-13/14); they are not "the" line — which is precisely why we originate our own.
