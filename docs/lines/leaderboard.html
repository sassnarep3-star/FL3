Leaderboard

The leaderboard rewards top-performing strategies on luck-adjusted, risk-controlled metrics. Raw profit alone never earns rewards — this is how luck is kept out of the equation. Reward tiers are proposals pending the owner's decision (Questions Q07, Suggestions S07).

Scoring metrics

IDMetricDescriptionSourcesFlag
LB01CLV (closing line value)Primary skill metric: difference between the price taken and the closing price, measured per bet. Rewards price-finding skill independent of outcome variance.
https://www.sharkbetting.com/blog/closing-line-value-clv-explained
LB02Edge % (edge over break-even)Hit probability minus break-even probability at the taken price; the expected edge per bet.
https://github.com/jcollins901/nfl-edge
LB03Risk-adjusted returnReturn measured against volatility and drawdown, so strategies are not rewarded for taking excessive risk.
https://www.sharkbetting.com/blog/closing-line-value-clv-explained
LB04Luck-adjusted efficiency scoreSignal quality measured with luck-adjusted efficiency inputs (INT/drop/fumble luck stripped), used for strategy-level ranking.
https://www.unexpectedpoints.com/p/luck-adjusted-quarterback-efficiency

Rules

IDRuleDescriptionSourcesFlag
LR01Gate-first eligibilityA strategy appears on the leaderboard only after passing all applicable validation gates (VG01-VG07). Gates are a prerequisite, not a ranking factor.
https://sharppicks.ai/tools/edge-calculator
LR02Minimum bet requirementStrategies must have a minimum number of scored bets in the period before ranking; strategies below the minimum are shown but not ranked or rewarded.
https://www.sharkbetting.com/blog/closing-line-value-clv-explained
LR03Walk-forward periodsRankings are computed over walk-forward periods using only data available at the time of each bet; no look-ahead.
https://github.com/jcollins901/nfl-edge
LR04Demotion on gate failureA strategy that fails a validation gate at any refresh point is demoted (suspended from rewards) until it passes a fresh walk-forward validation.
https://sharppicks.ai/tools/edge-calculator
LR05No strategy changes mid-periodA strategy's logic is frozen for the scoring period; any change resets its walk-forward clock and forfeits the current period's rewards.
https://github.com/jcollins901/nfl-edge
