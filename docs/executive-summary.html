Executive Summary

Purpose

FL3 is a research and decision-support system for generating original NFL betting lines. It develops signal strategies for single bets, parlays, and player-prop combinations; treats strategy performance as a leaderboard competition with rewards for top performers; requires every strategy to pass validation gates; determines correlation values; removes luck from performance measurement; and tracks injuries and lineup deficiencies (starters vs 2nd/3rd/4th-string backups) on both offense and defense.

What was verified (this session)

  • 2026 NFL standings — all 32 teams, transcribed line by line from the official NFL.com league standings page. Source: nfl.com/standings/league/2026/reg.
  • Week 10, 2026 injury report — 345 rows covering all 14 Week 10 games (28 teams; 4 teams on bye), transcribed line by line from the official NFL.com injury report. Source: nfl.com/injuries.
  • Official roster status — NFL.com team roster pages verified as the official source for roster status (ACT / DEV / PUP / TRD). Source: nfl.com team roster pages.
  • Betting-lines access check — official sportsbook pages were tested and are not machine-verifiable from this environment (BetMGM geo-wall, Caesars/FanDuel HTTP 500, DraftKings 404). All line values are therefore left blank and flagged unverified on the Market Data page.
  • Strategy methodology — signal families, validation gates, correlation handling, luck removal, and leaderboard design researched against authoritative analytics sources (FTN DVOA, Sharp Football Analysis, CLV research, luck-adjusted efficiency).

Key figures (verified)

  • League leaders at the Week 10 snapshot: Buffalo Bills and San Francisco 49ers (7-1, .875). Source: NFL.com standings.
  • Lowest: Los Angeles Chargers (0-8, .000). Source: NFL.com standings.
  • Week 10 injury report: 345 player entries across 14 games; 28 teams. Source: NFL.com injury report.
  • Lines: 14 Week 10 games tracked; all values blank & flagged (official books not machine-readable).

Decision state — what blocks action

  • Lines feed: no strategy can compute edge without verified official market prices. Suggestion S02 proposes the acquisition path; decision required.
  • Validation thresholds: gate numeric thresholds are proposals pending the owner's decision (Q03 / S03).
  • Depth-order data: depth-chart ordering is not machine-verifiable from NFL.com; a source is required before deficiency scoring (Q06 / S05 / FL02).
  • Jurisdiction: not confirmed; affects lines access and data licensing (Q10).
  • Leaderboard rewards: reward tiers are proposals pending decision (Q07 / S07).

How decisions are made here

No change is implemented without review. The workflow is: (1) read the open questions and gaps on the Questions & Answers page; (2) review the proposed paths on the Suggestions page; (3) choose the best path per decision; (4) implement only after selection. Flagged items are published on the Flagged for Review page with their source links.
