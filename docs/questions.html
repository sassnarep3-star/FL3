Questions and information gaps

The brief asks for a clear list of questions, information and answers that must be addressed before any decision can reasonably be made, based on what we have and what we do not have. This is that list. It is a table rather than prose because it is a work queue: each row states what we already have, exactly what is missing, how to get it, and which decision is waiting on it.

How to read the blocking column: yes = a decision that depends on this is currently undecidable with integrity (we would be guessing). no = it improves the model but does not gate the next step.

The seven that gate everything else

  • Q-01 market prices. We can build a number; we cannot yet say whether it is better than the

market's. Without opening and closing lines there is no CLV (gate G-03), no vig realism (G-06), and a leaderboard that ranks guesses. This is the only item on the list that is primarily a purchase.

  • Q-02 lineups. "Which backup is playing" is the heart of your brief. The league publishes a

designation, not a lineup ([FLG-017](flagged.html)), so every starter-quality adjustment in [models/line_model.yaml](model.html) is deliberately value: null.

  • Q-03 IR / transactions. A club can be missing its best player and show a nearly empty report

(Baltimore, Week 10 - [FLG-005](flagged.html)). Any deficiency count that reads only the report is systematically wrong in the direction of "everyone is healthy".

  • Q-04 game-level history. 33 of 137 verified 2026 games are in the repo, and no prior season

is. Every distribution (margin SD, home advantage, push rate, correlation) is therefore labelled provisional - see the fitted beta = 1.14 anomaly in [FLG-026](flagged.html), which is what a too-small sample looks like when you refuse to hide it.

  • Q-05/Q-06 calibration. The two constants that turn a rating gap into a spread, plus home-field

value. Right now: sigma_margin 11.92 pts (in-sample), alpha −0.82 pts (in-sample, not distinguishable from 0), and no schedule adjustment at all.

  • Q-07 correlation inputs. Every parlay and prop-combo price depends on a joint distribution, and

the only correlation we can currently measure with an interval is same-game home-vs-away points at r = 0.12 (CI −0.23 to +0.44) — i.e. uninformative. Until this is settled, gate G-07 cannot be evaluated and no multi-leg strategy can be promoted ([Correlation](correlation.html)).

Questions we should not ask yet

These look like questions but are actually consequences of the above, and asking them now invites a made-up answer:

  • "Which signal is best?" - no signal has a settled sample (G-01).
  • "Is our model sharper than the market?" - undefined until Q-01.
  • "How much should we bet?" - Kelly on an unmeasured edge is just a faster way to be wrong (Q-17).
  • "Are parlays +EV here?" - needs the correlation measurements in [Correlation](correlation.html),

which need Q-04 and Q-07.
