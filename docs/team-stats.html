Team efficiency inputs - official

Three pages from the league's own team-stats section, read 2026-11-14: offense passing (SRC-003), offense rushing (SRC-004), defense passing (SRC-005).

These are the inputs the line model is allowed to use today: volume (pass attempts, dropbacks, rush attempts), efficiency (yards per attempt, completion rate, sacks taken) and opponent-side reflections (what each defence gave up).

The column-label problem, and how it was solved

The static HTML renders 15 (passing) and 10 (rushing) numeric columns with no label row. Two steps made the mapping provable instead of plausible:

1. Per-row identities. Only columns whose value is reproducible from other columns were accepted: CMP% = CMP/ATT, YDS/ATT = YDS/ATT, 1ST% = 1st downs/ATT. 32 clubs × 8 rate columns all reconcile (V-06, V-07, V-08). Five of them sit on the .x5 rounding boundary and pass only with rounding slack - reported as a warning, not silently accepted (V-11). 2. League-wide identities. Every attempt faced by a defence is an attempt thrown by an offence, so the two independent tables must sum to the same number. They do, exactly:

The five clean identities are what prove the mapping. The two broken ones are what prove the discipline: a normal pipeline would have used pass_td and pass_int in a model and never noticed they came from tables that do not agree with each other. Those four columns are published but marked pricing-ineligible.

Withheld entirely (no label, no identity): rushing TD, fumbles, 20+/40+ plays, longest, and the trailing column on each table - 11 of 39 columns ([FLG-014](flagged.html)).

Where this is still weak

  • No games-played normalisation is available in the source, so clubs with 8 games and clubs with 9

are mixed in the totals; every rate here divides by that club's own games to stay honest.

  • Defence-side rushing and scoring pages are not what their names suggest:

/stats/team-stats/defense/scoring/ returns defensive touchdowns, not points allowed ([FLG-015](flagged.html)), so points allowed comes from the standings instead.

  • No player-level rates at all - which is the hard blocker for pricing individual props

([Q-08](questions.html)).

Every table on this site is generated from data/ by scripts/build_site.py; no value is typed into the HTML. Empty cells are deliberate: the value was not confirmable from an official source, so it is left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Nothing here is betting advice.
