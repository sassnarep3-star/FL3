Availability & depth — what the official report can and cannot tell you

Injury and availability tracking is where this project's most material data defect sits. Two captures of the same official Week 10 report disagree, and one of them silently drops a starting quarterback. That is documented here rather than smoothed over.

Why the second one matters. It holds only players who already carry a game designation. It is not a subset by accident: every one of its 160 rows has a status and none is blank, while the official page leaves 185 rows undesignated. Any feature built on it inherits that selection bias, and it cannot represent "on the report, no designation". It is the input to the model behind /board/. It omits Sam Darnold (QB, ribs/quadricep, Out) — a starting quarterback — so a model fed this file prices Seattle as if he were available. The owner of that track should switch to the complete capture or label every output as built on designated players only. This session did not edit their file.

What a "2nd-string replacement" can honestly mean here

The brief asks for depth deficiency: which clubs are starting a backup, and how deep the drop is. The honest position, unchanged from the earlier sessions and re-confirmed today, is:

  • Availability is official. The league injury report owns who is Out, Doubtful or Questionable, and the club roster page owns roster status (active / injured reserve / PUP). Both are league-published and both are linked.
  • Depth order is not official. The league publishes no depth ordinal. Where a club publishes a chart, it is club-published and carries no effective date, so it can establish an order but cannot be shown to be current.
  • Replacement evidence is not captured. Snap counts and the 90-minute inactive list are not on any league page this repo can reach. A replacement can be named; that he took the starter's snaps cannot be evidenced. This requirement stays open and is published as open.
  • The injury signal must be a surprise, not a level. A player listed as Questionable every week is not news. The only defensible version is the change against a dated prior capture — which requires a series this repo does not yet have, and whose capture protocol is in Method.

Why the practice-trend requirement cannot be back-filled

The official page renders one current report per week and overwrites the previous day's designations. There is no dated archive URL. A Wednesday-to-Friday progression therefore cannot be reconstructed for Week 10 — only captured prospectively, on a schedule, from now on. Stated as a gap rather than faked with an inference.
