Strategies, gates, and the scoreboard that cannot be gamed

A strategy earns a place on the leaderboard by passing pre-registered gates in order. It does not earn one by winning bets, and in this repository no bets exist: there is no licensed price, no stake, and no return. Everything here is a forecast-quality test.

Why combos are excluded outright

Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid when the legs are dependent, and it always overstates a combo's chance. Two conditions would have to hold before a combination could be ranked at all:

  1. a directly validated joint probability for the exact combination, not a product of marginals; and
  2. the operator's void, push and withdrawal rules, obtained in writing.

Neither holds here. There is no licensed operator, so condition 2 cannot be met at all. The correlation work published elsewhere in this repo states its measured r values and states plainly that they are not a joint model — which is correct, and is why no combo appears on this leaderboard.

Why ROI is not the ranking metric

Over a season, realised return is dominated by variance. A strategy that wins 7 of 11 is 11 coin flips; its ROI is a number with a confidence interval wider than the number. Ranking by it selects for luck. And in this repository it is not even computable, because no price was ever held. The replacement is proper-score skill with a game-cluster interval and a multiplicity adjustment — which measures whether the probabilities are good, which is the thing that would eventually produce a return.

Pre-registration, in force

The full frozen rule text is in PREREGISTRATION.md, with an executable form in scripts/preregister_model.py. The parts that make it a registration rather than a description: the constants, the sample window, the primary metric, the minimum detectable effect and the stopping rule were all fixed before any prospective forecast existed, and changing any of them creates a new version with a new commit rather than an edit.

Its own stopping rule, stated in advance: retire the model rather than re-fit it if the interval lower bound on Brier skill is below −0.05 after 200 scored singles. This is what stops a bad model from being tuned until it looks good.

What this is. A verified-data dashboard. Official league values carry a source link. Model output is labelled model output. No licensed betting price exists in this repository, so closing-line value and realised return are not computed and not implied anywhere.

Every page here is generated from the committed data files by scripts/build_verified_site.py and re-checked by scripts/check_verified_site.py. Other dashboards in this repository (root, board, docs, depth, site-9b181d31) are separate builds and are not superseded by this one.
