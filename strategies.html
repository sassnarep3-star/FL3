Strategies

Eight strategy specs, eight validation gates, five reward tiers. No strategy has a graded result yet, and this page says so instead of showing a number that does not exist.

Leaderboard status: live, empty. Grading requires completed games plus a stored pre-game line snapshot for each week. Only Week 10 is captured, and 13 of its 14 games have not been played as of 2026-11-14.

How the score works

score = sum over graded bets of (result x weight) / sqrt(n), with weight = declared edge / declared sigma, then divided by the largest unmeasured correlation haircut on the ticket.

  • Why sqrt(n): Stops a strategy from winning by volume alone. It must win per unit of evidence, not per unit of action.
  • Luck penalty: Any strategy whose graded P&L has |correlation with club luck_wins| > 0.30 has its score multiplied by 0.5 and is labelled luck-exposed.
  • Minimum to rank: 30 graded bets and at least 3 distinct weeks.
  • Abstention: A strategy that abstains is scored as a no-op, not as a win. Coverage is reported separately so abstention cannot be hidden.

What unlocks the leaderboard

  • capture each week's originated lines BEFORE kickoff (CI job, Monday 00:00 UTC)
  • capture final scores from an official NFL source after the week ends
  • grade every active strategy automatically in the same job
  • never edit a line snapshot after the fact — the file's git history is the audit trail

Rewards are allocated only to tier 3 and tier 4 strategies, in proportion to sqrt(n)-normalised score. A strategy that cannot beat a verifiable price earns nothing, no matter how good the narrative.
