Method in one paragraph

Values were transcribed from the official pages through the agent's fetch tool; the fetch tool does not retain raw response bytes, so the transcription is the capture record and is checked arithmetically by scripts/check_freshness.py (PCT = (W + 0.5·T)/(W+L+T), NET = PF − PA, sum(PF) = sum(PA), deltas against each club's Week 10 game score). Where the official page does not state a value, the cell is blank and the item is listed under Findings. Nothing was inferred or corrected.
