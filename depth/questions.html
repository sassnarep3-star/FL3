decision-blocking questions

What must be answered before deciding anything

Before any decision can be made reasonably, these questions have to be closed. Each one states what we already know, what we specifically do not know, what evidence would settle it, and what breaks if it stays open. 11 of 13 are open.

Why a question instead of a guess. Every entry below marks a place where the data ran out. Filling those places with a plausible number is the single easiest way to make this whole project worthless, so each one is recorded as an open question with the specific evidence that would close it.

Q-01 · What home-field advantage should the line model use? open

Area: Line origin  ·  Blocks: STR-01, all spread lines
We know
  • nfl.com publishes home and road win-loss records for all 32 teams (S01).
  • The repo has 32 teams and 274 team-games of PF/PA.
We do not know
  • Home and road points scored/allowed by team.
  • Any league-wide estimate of HFA for 2026.
Evidence needed
  • A source publishing home/away point splits (nfl.com standings does not).
  • Or compute it once per-team game logs are available.
Answer
open — deliberately unanswered rather than assumed
If unanswered
Every modelled line stays compressed toward pick'em and is not comparable to a market line.

Q-05 · How should disagreements between Ourlads and the official NFL report be governed? proposed-answer

Area: Depth charts  ·  Blocks: STR-03, backup-deficiency index
We know
  • 14 disagreements were found in Week 10 alone (data/depth_risk.json -> conflicts).
  • The official report is the league's own publication.
We do not know
  • Whether the NFL publishes a machine-readable depth chart at all.
Evidence needed
  • Confirm whether an official depth-chart feed exists.
Answer
Proposed and implemented: the official NFL report governs availability; Ourlads governs depth ORDER only. Every disagreement is published. A reviewer should confirm or overrule this.
If unanswered
Without a rule the two sources silently contradict each other 14 times a week.

Q-07 · Which correlations are real, and at what value? open

Area: Correlation  ·  Blocks: G7, STR-05, STR-06, all parlays
We know
  • The engine prices two-leg parlays with a Gaussian copula and refuses to price when rho is unset.
  • At rho=0.6, two 55% legs move from 30.25% (naive) to 40.35% (copula).
We do not know
  • Any empirical rho - none has been estimated from data in this repo.
Evidence needed
  • Per-player game logs to estimate rho for each proposed pair.
Answer
open — deliberately unanswered rather than assumed
If unanswered
Parlays stay unpriced rather than mispriced.

Q-08 · What is the pre-registered rule for calling a strategy 'skill based'? proposed-answer

Area: Luck removal  ·  Blocks: G8, all promotion decisions
We know
  • Six procedures (L1-L6) are specified in data/strategies.json.
We do not know
  • The pass threshold for each, and who signs off.
Evidence needed
  • Agreed thresholds before any results are seen - pre-registration is the point.
Answer
Proposed: all of L1, L4 and L5 must pass, plus a positive bootstrap 5th percentile. A reviewer should ratify or change this BEFORE results exist.
If unanswered
Thresholds chosen after seeing results are not evidence.
