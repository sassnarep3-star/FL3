Scope limits, stated plainly

  • 27 of the 28 playing teams are transcribed. Indianapolis is missing from the depth analysis (flagged F-06).
  • Draft pedigree is a proxy. A seventh-round starter who has played well is scored as a weak replacement. No snap counts or player grades exist in this repo (flagged F-09).
  • Questionable is not out. The site cannot know whether a Questionable player suited up, because the inactives list was not captured. That is question Q-03.
  • Injuries are not yet in the line. The exposure score has never been validated against outcomes, so it is deliberately absent from the line model (flagged F-05).
