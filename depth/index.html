What this is

The research question

Can we originate our own NFL lines from verified public data, and can we prove — not assume — that a strategy has skill rather than luck? This site is that question made auditable: source, method, gate, decision.

The honest answer so far

The data pipeline works and the gates are defined. No strategy has been validated, because no bets have been settled and no odds exist to compare against. The leaderboard is empty on purpose.
