Original model lines for Week 10

Thirteen original lines, generated from this repo's verified season data and nothing else. These are originated numbers, not market prices. No sportsbook figure was read, stored or copied — none was reachable.

Why these numbers are not yet bettable. Home-field advantage is 0.0 because no home/away scoring split exists in the data. No injury adjustment is applied because the exposure index has never been validated. There is no market comparison because there is no odds feed. Each of those is a deliberate, documented omission rather than a guess.

The model, in full

Inputs

  • League points per team-game: 22.96 — computed from the data, not assumed
  • Shrinkage prior k: 4.0 games — a convention, flagged F-10
  • Home-field advantage: 0.0 — uncalibrated, flagged F-04
  • Injury adjustment: 0.0 — unvalidated, flagged F-05

Formula

  • off = w·(PF/G) + (1−w)·μ, w = G/(G+k)
  • expected = μ + (own_off − μ) + (opp_def − μ)
  • home line = −(exp_home − exp_away)
  • total = exp_away + exp_home

The shrunken rates keep the league mean, so the deviation form above is algebraically the classic (own_off + opp_def)/2 once both are on a per-team-game scale.

What would make these lines trustworthy

  • A calibrated home-field term (Q-01). Until then every line is compressed toward pick'em.
  • A validated injury adjustment (Q-03, F-05). The exposure index exists; it has not earned a place in the line yet.
  • Any market price at all (Q-04). Without closing lines there is no way to test whether a model line is good.
  • An honest scoring rule — mean absolute error against the closing line would be the first real test, and it is impossible today.
