What the luck column means

Expected wins come from Pythagorean expectation: PF2.37 / (PF2.37 + PA2.37), multiplied by games played. Luck is (W + T/2) − expected wins. The half-win tie rule is quoted from the official tie-breaking page. tie-breaking page

The exponent 2.37 is a declared model constant, not a league figure. This decomposition cannot see turnovers, defensive touchdowns, or opponent field-goal misses.
